# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Criticise work, not people. Assume the other person is trying
to get it right.

## What we expect

- **Say what you mean.** Direct technical disagreement is welcome and useful. "This is wrong,
  and here is the case that breaks it" is a contribution.
- **Separate the claim from the person.** A bad argument is a bad argument; the person making
  it is a colleague.
- **Accept correction, and offer it plainly.** Being wrong in public is a normal part of
  engineering. Nobody should have to defend a position they no longer hold.
- **Respect a decision once it is made**, and reopen it with new evidence rather than by
  repetition.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, whether public or private.
- Personal attacks, demeaning remarks, and comments about a person's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, repeating a settled argument, or making a project
  space unpleasant enough that people stop participating.

## Scope

Everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
space where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **oss@kecku.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.

Tell us what happened, where, and when. You do not need to prove anything or write a formal
complaint; a description is enough to start.

We will acknowledge within five working days. We will tell you what we decided and why. If we
decide not to act, we will say so and give the reason rather than letting the report go quiet.

## Enforcement

What we do depends on what happened and whether it is a pattern. In rough order: a private
word, a public correction, a temporary ban from project spaces, a permanent one.

Maintainers who do not hold to this are subject to it in the same way. A code of conduct that
exempts the people with commit access is not one.
